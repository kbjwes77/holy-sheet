Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Grade Lake turns a test into printable answer sheets and grades scanned or photographed sheets. This policy explains what we collect to do that, who handles it, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form.
  • Your library: the study materials, groups and tags you create, and whether each is a draft, private or published. Published materials are visible to, and can be copied by, other signed-in users, along with your display name as the author.
  • Sessions: a sign-in cookie that lasts 30 days from your last visit. Only a hash of its token is stored on our server.
  • Usage records: events such as signing in, grading runs and plan changes, used to run the service and enforce plan limits.
  • Grading uploads: the scans or photos of answer sheets you upload to the grader, and the results produced from them: each student's name, score and percent, written answers as they were read, the grading feedback, and any curve you apply. Student names written on sheets are part of these uploads.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive and store only your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the plan, its status and its renewal date. We never see your card number.
  • Visits: a cookie of our own holding a random ID, which lasts about 13 months, so we can count visits and see how people find Grade Lake. With it we record the pages you view, the website that linked you here (its domain only, such as google.com), and any campaign tags in the link you followed. When you sign up or sign in, your visits are linked to your account. We do not track browsers that send a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.
  • On your device: your light or dark theme choice and a few printing preferences, kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

How we use it

To provide the service: sign you in, store your library, print sheets, grade uploads, and handle your subscription. We use your email address to send account emails: a welcome message when you sign up, password-reset and email-confirmation links when you ask for them, and notices about changes to your account. We do not send marketing email. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • AI model providers. Reading handwriting and grading free-response answers is done by AI models reached through OpenRouter. When you grade, images of the name box and of handwritten answers, and the question text, model answer and rubric needed to grade them, are sent to those providers. Filled bubbles are read by Grade Lake itself and are not sent. Because student work is involved, make sure you have the right to share it with a grading service under your school's rules.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers our account emails. It receives your email address and display name, and the content of each message we send you.
  • Hosting. Grade Lake runs on a cloud server, where your data is stored.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Records of the pages viewed are deleted after about 13 months, and so are visitor IDs not linked to an account.
  • Uploaded scans and photos, and the diagnostic images made from them, are removed from the server automatically about two hours after a grading job.
  • The results of each grading run are saved to your Results page and kept until you delete them there, or until your account is deleted.
  • Your account and library stay until you delete your account.
  • Billing records may be kept by Stripe and by us as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your materials and saved grading results at any time, make them private, and manage or cancel your subscription from the Pricing page. From the Account page you can change your name, email address and password, or delete your account and everything tied to it: your library, anything you published, and your saved grading results. Deleting an account ends its subscription immediately, and its visits are no longer linked to it; we keep only anonymous counts. You can also ask us to send you a copy of your data. Signing out removes the session cookie from your browser.

Children

Grade Lake is for teachers and tutors, not for children. Do not create an account if you are under 13. Teachers are responsible for any student information they upload.

Security

Sessions use HttpOnly cookies, passwords are hashed, password-reset links work once and expire after an hour, and the site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell signed-in users.
