Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Grade Lake turns a test into printable answer sheets and grades scanned or photographed sheets. This policy explains what we collect to do that, who handles it, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form.
  • Your library: the study materials, groups and tags you create, and whether each is a draft, private or published. Published materials are visible to, and can be copied by, other signed-in users, along with your display name as the author.
  • Sessions: a sign-in cookie that lasts 30 days from your last visit. Only a hash of its token is stored on our server.
  • Usage records: events such as signing in, grading runs and plan changes, used to run the service and enforce plan limits.
  • Grading uploads: the scans or photos of answer sheets you upload to the grader, and the grades produced from them. Student names written on sheets are part of these uploads.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive and store only your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the plan, its status and its renewal date. We never see your card number.
  • On your device: your light or dark theme choice and a few generator preferences, kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

To provide the service: sign you in, store your library, generate sheets, grade uploads, and handle your subscription. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • AI model providers. Reading handwriting and grading free-response answers is done by AI models reached through OpenRouter. When you grade, images of the name box and of handwritten answers, and the question text, model answer and rubric needed to grade them, are sent to those providers. Filled bubbles are read by Grade Lake itself and are not sent. Because student work is involved, make sure you have the right to share it with a grading service under your school's rules.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Grade Lake runs on a cloud server, where your data is stored.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded scans and grading files are removed from the server automatically about two hours after a grading job, or sooner when you discard it. Download your CSV before then.
  • Your account and library stay until you ask us to delete them.
  • Billing records may be kept by Stripe and by us as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your materials at any time, make them private, and manage or cancel your subscription from the Pricing page. You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it, or to send you a copy of it. Signing out removes the session cookie from your browser.

Children

Grade Lake is for teachers and tutors, not for children. Do not create an account if you are under 13. Teachers are responsible for any student information they upload.

Security

Sessions use HttpOnly cookies, passwords are hashed, and the site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell signed-in users.
