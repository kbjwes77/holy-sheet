Grade Lake / Gradescope alternative

Comparison

A Gradescope alternative for individual teachers

Gradescope is built for institutions and courses. Grade Lake is built for one teacher with a stack of papers. This page compares them fairly and says when you should stay with Gradescope.

Updated October 27, 2026

The short version

If your school or department already licenses Gradescope, or you need collaborative grading across a teaching team, single sign-on or course-wide analytics, Gradescope is the better fit. If you are a teacher or tutor who wants to write a test, print it, scan the stack and get scores without an institutional rollout, Grade Lake is a lighter, self-serve option.

When Gradescope is the better choice

  • Your institution already has an Institutional license.
  • Several instructors or teaching assistants grade the same exam and need to share rubrics and grading work.
  • You need single sign-on or want to connect grading to a learning management system.
  • You grade long, complex, multi-page work such as university problem sets.

When Grade Lake is the better choice

  • You work alone. Nothing to roll out: build a test in the free generator, print it, and start grading with a free trial.
  • Short handwritten answers. A model answer and rubric are all the AI needs. It proposes scores and you review them.
  • Quizzes written with AI. Have a chatbot write the questions and paste them in.
  • A fixed, small price. $5 a month or $40 a year, shown on the pricing page.

Switching or trying both

The two work on different sheets, so a test you have printed for one cannot be graded by the other. Try Grade Lake on a single quiz: the free generator needs no account and costs nothing to print.

Questions, answered

Is Gradescope free?

Gradescope lists a free Basic plan and an Institutional plan with pricing available by contact form. The Institutional plan includes features the Basic plan does not, such as collaborative grading, AI-powered grading and single sign-on.

Can a single teacher use Grade Lake without their school?

Yes. It is a self-serve web app. Printing is free, and grading is a $5 monthly or $40 annual Pro plan with a 7-day free trial.

Does Grade Lake work with a learning management system?

Not today. Scores export as a CSV that you can import into your gradebook.

Can I grade handwritten work with Grade Lake?

Yes, short written answers. An AI model reads the handwriting and proposes a score against your model answer and rubric, and you review before exporting.
