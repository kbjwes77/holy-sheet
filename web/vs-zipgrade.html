Grade Lake / ZipGrade alternative

Comparison

A ZipGrade alternative that also grades handwriting

ZipGrade is a well-known, inexpensive bubble-sheet scanner. Grade Lake overlaps with it on multiple choice and goes further on written answers and test creation. Here is an even-handed comparison so you can pick the one that fits.

Updated October 27, 2026

The short version

If you only need to scan multiple-choice sheets, want native phone apps or need to work offline, ZipGrade is excellent value, and at $6.99 a year it is cheaper than Grade Lake. Choose Grade Lake if you want handwritten answers read and scored against a rubric, quizzes written with an AI chatbot, graphs or tables inside questions, or answer sheets built around your own test.

When ZipGrade is the better choice

  • You grade only multiple choice and want the lowest cost: the free plan covers 100 papers a month and the paid plan is about seven dollars a year.
  • You want to grade in a classroom with a phone and no internet connection.
  • You already have a routine with its standard answer-sheet forms and do not need anything else.

When Grade Lake is the better choice

  • Handwritten answers. Written-answer questions print as writing boxes, and an AI model reads the handwriting and proposes a score from your model answer and rubric. You review every transcription before export.
  • Writing the test is part of the tool. Type questions into the free generator or have ChatGPT or Claude write them and paste the result. Each question prints with its own bubbles instead of on a generic form.
  • Figures. Put graphs and tables beside the questions that use them.
  • A library. Keep tests in groups, reprint them next term and share them with other teachers.

Can I use ZipGrade forms with Grade Lake?

No. Grade Lake's sheets carry a QR code and corner marks made for its own grader, and ZipGrade cannot read them. You can still use the free generator to print tests and mark them by hand.

Questions, answered

Is Grade Lake cheaper than ZipGrade?

No. ZipGrade's paid plan is listed at $6.99 for one year and its free plan scans up to 100 papers a month. Grade Lake Pro is $5 a month or $40 a year. Printing sheets with Grade Lake is free.

Does ZipGrade grade handwritten answers?

Its pricing page does not list written-answer grading. Grade Lake reads handwritten answers with an AI model and proposes scores that you review.

Can I switch from ZipGrade to Grade Lake?

Yes, but you recreate your tests, since the two apps use different answer sheets. The free generator and an AI chatbot can rebuild a test from your questions quickly.

Does Grade Lake have a phone app?

No. It is a web app that works in a browser, and you can upload scans or phone photos of the sheets.
